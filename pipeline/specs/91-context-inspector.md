#91 — Context-sensitive inspector

Start implementation only after journal90's closing checkpoint passes exact CI.
The [full thirty-idea roadmap](../research-platform-roadmap.md) remains active.

## Acceptance

1. Reuse current schema-bound controls and owner undo/verification. Selecting a
   supported axis,uncertainty element or whole figure presents relevant settings
   and a visible selection context; preserve access to advanced canonical intent.
2. Actual renderer-owned image geometry supplies supported hit regions and binds
   them to the current artifact SHA/dimensions/revision. Existing text-target
   geometry is not evidence of interval or axis-element hit coverage.
3. Figure/panel/source identity stays explicit; unavailable/partial/unmapped
   elements cannot guess another panel's settings. Switching among text editing,
   diagnostics and inspector preserves their reviewed draft/selection semantics.
4. Keyboard controls and desktop/mobile paths can select contexts,edit supported
   values,undo and perform real native re-verification. Selection alone changes
   neither scientific intent nor verification status. Later edits invalidate old
   hit regions,replies and current evidence.
5. Actual source/minimum/fresh installed browser/artifact/refusal controls and all
   contribution gates prove the completed scope. Push coherent milestones and
   verify exact14/three-workflow CI before advancing. Keep benchmark originals
   and recorded failure outcomes intact; no new performance advantage is implied.

## Existing foundations

web/controls.js owns stable field paths,bindings,flush and sync. preview_geometry.py
captures actual text boxes at the final cropped PNG draw; text editing and
diagnostics already have separate interaction modes and current-image fences.
Journal90 demonstrates valid-context identity guards. Extend these mechanisms,
do not replace working editors with a second schema or mutation path.
